# AI Chatbot & Voice-Agent Disclosure Laws — Watch Note

> **Status:** verify-first watch list, not legal authority. Last reviewed 2026-10-13 by the KRASA landscape monitor. Scope definitions and carve-outs vary by state and were not fully confirmed from enrolled bill text; treat every row as "check before relying on it."

Used by: `skills/admin/ai-customer-contact-compliance-reviewer.md`, `skills/customer-service/ai-phone-receptionist-script.md`, and any skill that drafts website chat or automated text/voice flows.

## Why a shop should care

Most 2025–2026 state chatbot laws target "companion" bots and exclude ordinary customer-service bots. A few are written more broadly — around any conversational AI service open to the public — which could reach a shop's AI phone receptionist or website chat. Effective dates cluster on **2027-01-01**, so the window to fix greeting scripts is Q4 2026.

## House rule for skills that read this note

1. Default every AI voice/chat greeting to a plain AI-identity disclosure, regardless of state — it costs nothing and satisfies the strictest version of every law above.
2. Never script an AI agent to deny being AI or to imply it is a licensed professional.
3. Do not quote a bill number, penalty, or effective date to a customer from this note; route specifics to owner/counsel.
4. Separate federal layers (TCPA consent for automated calls/texts, FCC treatment of AI-generated voices) are handled in the compliance reviewer skill and are not restated here.
